# Curator’s research notes

Five exhibits let visitors compare different fates within a short visit. Apollo 15 illustrates a vehicle parked after its crew finished fieldwork. LCROSS made a planned impact part of an experiment. Opportunity examined Mars by traveling across it, while InSight investigated the planet from one location. Voyager 1 adds a traveling spacecraft whose planetary encounters led into an extended scientific mission.

## Other subjects considered

| Candidate | What a visitor could learn | Primary record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Surveyor 3 | Apollo 12 astronauts recovered parts of a robotic lander. Testing its television camera on Earth revealed how materials weathered on the Moon. | [NASA mission history](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/surveyor-3/) and [NASA camera analysis](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19710024418) |
| Apollo lunar modules | The descent stage became a launch platform for the astronauts’ ascent stage. It is a useful contrast with scientific instruments that crews deliberately deployed. | [NASA lunar historic sites inventory](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/nasa-usg-lunar-historic-sites-reva.pdf) |
| Apollo laser reflectors | Observatories time a pulse of light traveling to the Moon and back. A passive device on the ground can still help scientists take measurements from Earth. | [NASA’s experiment history](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/apollo/apollo-11/the-apollo-experiment-that-keeps-on-giving/) |
| Sojourner | NASA’s first Mars rover traveled with Pathfinder. It offers a bridge to the larger rovers that followed. | [NASA Pathfinder record](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-pathfinder/) |
| Spirit | Soft soil trapped Spirit. Engineers gave the rover a stationary science role before communications ended. | [NASA/JPL mission update](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasas-mars-rover-spirit-starts-a-new-chapter/) and [NASA end-of-mission report](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20160001767) |
| Phoenix | The lander dug into ice-rich soil. Students could compare its direct sampling with the remote observations of LCROSS’s lunar impact. | [NASA Phoenix record](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/mars-phoenix/) |
| Pioneer 10 | Its final signal reached Earth in 2003. It continued on its outward path, a comparison with Voyager 1 that separates motion from radio contact. | [NASA Pioneer 10 record](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/pioneer-10/) |

These sources informed exhibit selection. The dedicated source section in the museum identifies the references used for the five published mission files.
